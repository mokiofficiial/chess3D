Changing opponent, strength, side or clock starts a new game.

How to play

  • Tap a piece, then tap a glowing square to move it.
  • Drag to turn the board, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Every piece captures in its own way. Watch for it.
  • The moves are written on the scoresheet beside the board.
  • Checkmate the king and the whole board goes up in flames.
  • Graphics, clock and sound live in Settings.
